vega-webgpu

Version 2.0.1

A patch for 2.0.0: a stroked rect no longer draws its fill under the outer half of its stroke.

GitHub release Render gallery

Get it

This build targets Vega 6. It came out before vega-webgpu was published to npm, so there is no package for it. The bundles are attached to its GitHub release.

Fixes

  • A rect or group with square corners and a stroke stops its fill at its own edge, where the fill reached under the outer half of the stroke: the antialiased edge came out darker, and a translucent stroke showed the fill through it
